About

Founded in 2014. Led hands-on.

Amrot is a technology consultancy founded by Araya Kebede — an engineer who has spent his career using diverse technologies to automate and streamline how organizations work.

That career began in web GIS in 2003 and runs through mission-critical systems for the U.S. Department of Labor, DOT, FEMA, NOAA, the Department of Justice, the National Science Foundation, the U.S. Forest Service, and — today — the DC Department of Public Works.

It's a deliberately small, senior practice: you work directly with the person who architects and builds the solution. No layers, no hand-offs — just objective, practical engineering that leaves your agency better off and in control of its own systems.
